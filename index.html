Who this is for

For the highly effective operator who's winning by every external measure — and privately suspects they're leaving their real shot on the table.

  • You've been telling yourself you'll figure it out after the next raise, the next launch, the next reorg.

Clarity has time value.

24 hours. One night outside. A north star that holds.

You go to a private site in West Marin. Phone, watch, laptop, books — none of it comes with you. Neither do meetings, Slack, or anyone else's opinions.

You get a structured journal, gear, food, and a field team on site. You don't get advice, distractions, or a way to hide.

What you get instead is enough time and enough silence to hear what you actually think.

One night solo under the stars with structured contemplation of what you actually value. You leave with a life-path framework — a five-value compass and a decision filter you can run every future move through.

Solo, in community

The 24 hours are yours alone. The hours before and after are not.

You'll arrive to a room of eleven other operators at similar altitude — the kind of people whose opinion you'd actually want. You'll break bread together, set intentions together, and come back from the solo to pressure-test what you found in front of the group. Real feedback, no advice.

You leave with your own north star, sharpened against eleven others — and a cohort you can call on long after the fire is out.

Questions

Is this a retreat?

No. A retreat is designed to make you feel good. This is designed to give you a direction. Comfortable, but not comforting.

Do I need to be in some kind of crisis?

No. Most people who come are doing well by every external measure. That's part of the problem — success without direction is its own trap.

What if I already have a plan?

Good. Bring it. Pressure-test it against something bigger than the plan itself.

Will there be group therapy?

No. The group exists to pressure-test your thinking, not to process feelings. You'll be with peers, not patients.

Who else will be there?

Eleven other operators at similar altitude. We screen for fit and for range — you won't be the only person from your industry, and you won't be surrounded by them either.

What does it cost?

Pricing and dates go out with the acceptance email after we've had a conversation.

What if I get accepted and something changes?

Tell us. We hold seats for people who commit, so we ask you to commit for real — but life happens, and we'd rather know early.
